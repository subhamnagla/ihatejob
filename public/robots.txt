# Everything here is free to read, by people and by machines alike.
#
# There are no per-crawler rules below, and that is deliberate. robots.txt is
# matched by the single most specific User-agent group, not by all of them
# together: the moment a named group exists for GPTBot or ClaudeBot, that
# agent stops reading the "*" group entirely - including its Disallow lines.
# A file that lists twenty AI crawlers under "Allow: /" to look welcoming is
# a file that has quietly opened /admin to all twenty of them.
#
# "*" already permits every crawler that honours this file, AI crawlers
# included, so that is all it says.

User-agent: *
Allow: /
Disallow: /admin
Disallow: /api/

Sitemap: https://ihatejob.app/sitemap.xml

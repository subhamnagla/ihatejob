Saving as PDF

You are viewing a sample CV, not your own — printing now saves the sample. Choose “Back to mine” first if that is not what you meant.

Your browser's print dialog does the export. Two settings matter:

  1. Set Destination to Save as PDF.
  2. Set Margins to None and turn Background graphics on, so colours and spacing match the preview.

Coming from LinkedIn?

Fastest — your profile as a PDF. Open your own LinkedIn profile and press Resources — or More, since LinkedIn shows one or the other depending on the account. Choose Save to PDF and drop the file above. On a phone: your photo → View Profile → ⋯ → Save to PDF.

More complete — your data archive. LinkedIn → Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data. Drop the .zip above when it arrives. Fuller data, but a profile-only request takes a few minutes and the whole archive can take up to 48 hours. Only the profile files inside are opened — your messages and connections are never read.

There is no “connect to LinkedIn” button because no LinkedIn API gives out your positions or education. Nothing here signs in to anything.

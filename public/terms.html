What this is

ihatejob is a CV builder and CV checker that runs in your browser. It is free, there is no account, there is no paid tier, and every feature is available to everyone. The source code is published under the MIT licence.

What the rating is, and what it is not

The score out of 100, the six grades and the comparison against a job advert are opinions produced by rules written into this site. They are a second reading of your own document, not a professional assessment, not careers advice, and not a prediction.

Nothing here is a promise of an interview, a job, or that any employer or applicant tracking system will read your CV in a particular way. A perfect score is not a guarantee of anything, and a poor one is not a verdict on you.

If you leave the box unticked, it is read and not published. You can ask us to take down anything of yours at any time.

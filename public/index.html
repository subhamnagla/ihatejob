Already have a LinkedIn profile?

Then you have already written most of a CV. Bring it across in a minute — nothing here signs in to anything.

Fastest — your profile as a PDF

About a minute. The layout is read properly rather than guessed at.

  1. Open your own LinkedIn profile
  2. Press Resources — or More. LinkedIn shows one or the other depending on the account, so check for both
  3. Choose Save to PDF
  4. Drop the file into the builder

On a phone: tap your photo → View Profile → ⋯ → Save to PDF.

More complete — your data archive

Fuller dates, descriptions and education. You wait for it, though.

  1. LinkedIn → Settings & Privacy
  2. Data privacy → Get a copy of your data
  3. Tick the profile categories, or request the whole archive
  4. Drop the .zip in when the email arrives

A profile-only request usually arrives in minutes. The full archive can take up to 48 hours. Only the profile files inside are opened — your messages and connections are never read.

Import from LinkedIn

There is no “Connect with LinkedIn” button here, and that is not an oversight: no LinkedIn API hands out your own positions or education. Anything that claims to do it is asking for your password. Your file is read in your browser and never uploaded.
